# Miles or cashback? Start with what you value

Five-minute guided presentation. All flight examples below are **one-way, one person, Singapore-origin**. This is content and a calculator specification, not a built slide deck or deployed calculator.

## Slide 1 — What is the experience worth to you? (0:00–0:50)
Show the calculator QR/short link once a working site exists. Ask participants to choose Perth or Tokyo and enter how much extra they would pay for business rather than the economy trip they would actually buy. Do not show retail business fares first.

Illustrative one-way upgrade willingness: Perth S$100; Tokyo S$150; Heathrow backup S$500. These are presenter assumptions, not market values.

## Slide 2 — What do the miles buy? (0:50–1:30)

| Singapore to | Economy Saver | Business Saver | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Perth | 20,500 | 42,500 | Main lower-mileage example |
| Tokyo, Haneda or Narita | 25,500 | 54,500 | Main relatable example |
| London Heathrow | 44,000 | 108,500 | Backup long-haul example |

All rates are one-way full award tickets, not upgrade awards. Taxes and charges are additional; availability is capacity-controlled.[15]

**Illustrative cash inputs, NOT live quotations:** economy alternative Perth S$300 / Tokyo S$450 / Heathrow S$700; award cash charges S$100 / S$100 / S$100 respectively. These are independent teaching assumptions, not a claim that one-way fares or taxes equal half a return ticket. In particular, outbound Singapore–Heathrow charges are not the same as UK-origin charges. Replace with matched dated quotations before describing these as real travel prices.

For someone taking a return holiday, compare the cost of the entire actual itinerary: one-way cash tickets can differ substantially from half a return fare. The calculator must allow the economy fare avoided to be the actual incremental saving, including any change to the remaining flight.

## Slide 3 — How would you earn them? (1:30–2:20)
Citi Rewards: 4 mpd on eligible online and shopping spending; approximately S$1,000 eligible spend per statement month. Travel-related and mobile-wallet transactions are excluded from the online 10X benefit; the bonus cap is 9,000 points per statement month.[13][9]
UOB Preferred Visa: bonus UNI$9 plus base UNI$1 per S$5. Separate bonus caps of UNI$1,080 for selected online spending and mobile contactless spending per calendar month equate to S$600 in each bucket at the headline rate. Account for S$5 transaction rounding.[22]
Main demo: S$1,000/month qualifying spending at 4 mpd = 4,000 miles/month. Continuous earning estimates: Perth 10.625 months; Tokyo 13.625 months; Heathrow 27.125 months. Starting balance and welcome bonus are zero. Transfer blocks, posting delays and expiry are not included; actual redemption-ready dates may be later.

Do not combine different bank points pools without separate transfer rules and fees.

## Slide 4 — What are you giving up? (2:20–3:35)
Cashback reference: UOB Absolute 1.7% on eligible ordinary purchases. Some categories earn 0.3%; eligible contactless spend currently gets an additional promotional rebate. Match the alternative to the same purchases.[3]

Illustrative defaults: 1.7% cashback; 15 extra minutes/month versus cashback; S$40/hour personal time value; S$30 total incremental transfer/other fee allowance; ongoing annual fees waived, otherwise add them. Zero welcome bonus.

Tokyo example: 54,500 miles / 4 = S$13,625 spend. Forgone cashback S$231.625; time cost S$136.25; award cash S$100; fee allowance S$30. Total economic cost S$497.875 (display S$497.88). Personal value = S$450 economy avoided + S$150 upgrade willingness = S$600. Net benefit S$102.125 (display S$102.13).

At 30 extra minutes/month, Tokyo total cost becomes S$634.125; break-even upgrade willingness S$184.125 (display S$184.13). At S$150 willingness the net benefit is minus S$34.125 (display minus S$34.13).

## Slide 5 — Your answer, not the airline's valuation (3:35–5:00)
Break-even upgrade willingness = award cash + forgone rewards + incremental fees + incremental time cost − actual economy fare avoided. Clamp displayed required willingness at zero, but explain any saving below the economy baseline.

| One-way scenario | Total cost | Break-even upgrade willingness | Example willingness | Net benefit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Perth | S$416.88 | S$116.88 | S$100 | −S$16.88 |
| Tokyo | S$497.88 | S$47.88 | S$150 | +S$102.13 |
| Heathrow, backup | S$862.38 | S$162.38 | S$500 | +S$337.63 |

Figures use the same assumptions above and decimal half-up rounding to cents. These are simplified nominal teaching comparisons, not forecasts or current market quotations. Perth is close enough to be sensitive to assumptions. Show risks of expiry, devaluation and inability to find suitable seats, rather than presenting a positive number as a guarantee.

Closing: Start with your own value. Compare with your real alternative. Count money and time. Then decide.

## Appendix — Public welcome-offer examples
Citi PremierMiles direct fee-paying welcome route: 30,000 bonus miles, S$196.20 fee, S$800 qualifying spend; official terms run to 31 March 2027.[18]
Fee-waived route: 8,000 bonus miles and S$800 qualifying spend. Qualifying period is approval month plus two calendar months; e.g. August approval through end October. Official terms run to 31 March 2027.[19]
Thus paying S$196.20 buys 22,000 extra bonus miles compared with the fee-waived route: about 0.89 cents per extra mile. Regular spending miles are separate. Eligibility and delayed award fulfilment apply.[18][19]
MoneySmart Citi Rewards route: S$400 cash via PayNow as the advertised later-applicant baseline, versus S$450 for the first 200 qualifying applicants; minimum S$500 qualifying spend within 30 days inclusive of approval date, plus claim requirements. Promotion 10–31 October 2026; new-to-Citi eligibility required. Do not assume it stacks with direct bank welcome offers.[13]
A cash welcome gift can accompany a miles-earning card. Compare application routes separately from ongoing rewards strategy. Do not automatically count S$400 as forgone just because someone chooses miles, and do not subtract it unless it is genuinely displaced.

## Delivery and calculator specification
Five short live slides; cards, welcome offers and sweet spots in optional help/appendix. Fields: destination, one-way upgrade willingness, monthly eligible spending, incremental minutes/month and hourly value. Editable advanced fields: economy saving, award cash, fees, effective cashback, card earn rate, caps, opening balances and mutually exclusive application routes.

Keep ongoing rewards strategy separate from welcome-offer selection. Do not treat already-earned miles as free or recreate historical acquisition costs without explaining the different prospective decision. Keep cash bonuses on miles cards available when eligible. No financial inputs collected publicly. Repository upload does not enable GitHub Pages or change repository visibility.
