03 · How would you earn them?

One pool. One cap.

The calculator uses one simple monthly eligible-spend pool. It does not combine bank points or optimize category caps.

Eligible spend / monthS$1,000Hypothetical one-pool cap
Illustrative earn rate4 mpd4,000 miles in a full month
Opening balance0Prospective earning only

Why does the calculator show more time?

  • Default transfer block: 10,000 miles; editable, with an idealised 1-mile switch.
  • Round the remaining award target up to a transfer block, then round accumulation up to full months.
  • Whole-month cashback and time costs include any miles earned beyond the award target. Leftover miles receive S$0 value.

Keep the earning pool simple

Effective monthly spend is the lower of eligible spend and the single cap. No multi-bank optimiser, category split, transaction rounding or exclusions are simulated.

Monthly eligible spend × miles per S$ = miles per month

This does not model opening miles; do not use it to price an existing balance. Choose a one-way trip that matches your actual plan.

04 · What do you give up?

Count the cash and the time.

Tokyo illustrates the default whole-month model. The result changes when your genuine alternative or earning conditions change.

Tokyo · 54,500-mile award

  • 54,500 required miles round up to a 60,000-mile transfer goal at the default 10,000-mile block.
  • At 4,000 miles per month, 15 whole months earn 60,000 miles; 5,500 remain after the award.
  • 15 months × S$1,000 × 1.7% = S$255 cashback forgone.
  • 15 months × 15 minutes × S$40/hour = S$150 time cost.

Fees and gift routes

S$100 hypothetical award cash + S$30 hypothetical fee allowance.

A welcome fee or a genuinely displaced cash offer belongs in the cost. A cash gift that can accompany the miles card belongs on the value side.

Cashback + time + award cash + incremental fees = modeled cost

Surplus miles are shown separately and valued at S$0. Credit delay affects the ready-date estimate in parallel; no extra monthly spend or time cost is assumed during that wait.

All cash amounts and personal time values are illustrative, not quotes or universal valuations. Offer eligibility and qualifying spend are not checked.

05 · Your answer, not the airline's valuation

Start with your own break-even.

Under the Tokyo defaults, the whole-month estimate is simple enough to audit—and sensitive to your own numbers.

Total modeled costS$535
Break-even extra willingnessS$85
Example willingness S$150 → net+S$65

Use your own real alternative

The S$450 economy saving plus S$150 willingness gives S$600 personal value. Subtract S$535 modeled cost. This is a teaching comparison, not a booking recommendation.

Break-even willingness = max(0, total cost − economy saving − miles-card cash gift)

Before deciding

  • Positive does not guarantee a seat, savings, or a future award rate.
  • Check expiry, devaluation risk, real dates, cash charges and full-trip fares.
  • Within S$50 of zero? Small assumption changes can reverse the sign.
