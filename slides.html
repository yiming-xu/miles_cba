03 · How would you earn them?

A welcome bonus, then a long climb.

The calculator starts at zero miles and uses one eligible-spend pool, one cap and whole earning months—not a multi-bank optimiser. It assumes all entered S$1,000 monthly spend earns 4 mpd.

Default earning calculation

  • After the assumed 16,000 welcome miles, 38,500 miles remain.
  • At 4,000 miles per S$1,000 spend month, that takes 10 whole earning months.
  • Default transfer block is an idealised 1 mile; real programs can require larger blocks.

Welcome terms are not automatic

Citi Rewards offers 40,000 ThankYou points (16,000 miles) with S$800 qualifying spend by the end of the second calendar month after approval. The bonus must be fulfilled within three calendar months after the spend period; terms through 31 March 2027. Official terms ↗

The model assumes all S$1,000 monthly spend earns 4 mpd; 4 mpd is limited to eligible online/shopping spend, excluding online travel and mobile wallets. The 9,000 bonus-point statement cap and transaction exclusions are not simulated.

A 10-month earning estimate is not a ready-date or seat-availability forecast. From the 20 October 2026 research snapshot, 224 days remain to 1 June 2027; this earning plan cannot build the target in time. The estimate treats the 16,000-mile bonus as available; qualifying spend and bonus posting are not modeled, and posting could shorten the runway further.

04 · What do you give up?

Count the cash and the time.

For the same SIN–HND example, use the same whole-month model as the calculator.

Opportunity cost over 10 earning months

  • Cashback forgone: S$1,000 × 1.7% × 10 = S$170.00.
  • Time: 15 minutes × S$40/hour × 10 = S$100.00.
  • Award cash charges: S$100.00 (hypothetical).
  • General fees: S$30.00; no separate offer fee in the default scenario.

Keep unlike items separate

The S$450 economy amount is an unverified assumed alternative—not a live fare. The S$150 willingness is personal. The S$800 qualifying spend is a condition, not bonus earning automatically included in monthly spend.
