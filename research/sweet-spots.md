# SQ KrisFlyer sweet spots — one-way, Singapore-origin

Scope: SQ-operated flights redeemable with KrisFlyer. Standard rates verified against SQ's official chart. Sweet-spot descriptions are a reasoned comparison, not proof of live inventory. No date-specific award seat search was performed. Cash charges and real cash alternatives must be checked separately.

## Useful standard-rate candidates

| Route | Business Saver miles | Why consider it | Negative case |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Perth / Darwin | 42,500 | Lower absolute miles target than Tokyo or eastern Australia | Shorter flight; little personal value if economy is comfortable and cheap |
| Istanbul | 68,000 | Less mileage than Heathrow's 108,500; meaningful long-haul experience | Not London: onward travel, accommodation, time and separate-ticket risk can erase savings |
| Johannesburg / Cape Town | 68,000 | Same favourable Zone 10 rate for a long-haul destination | Only useful if you actually want South Africa; Cape Town routing may involve Johannesburg |
| Sydney / Melbourne / Auckland / Christchurch | 72,000 | More time to enjoy a business seat than a short regional trip | Larger miles target; cheap economy alternative may still win |
| Beijing / Shanghai | 45,000 | Lower mileage than Tokyo's 54,500 | Flight/product choice and personal destination preference matter |

All listed standard rates come from the SQ Saver chart.[15] Suitesmile independently identifies these regions as sweet-spot candidates, including Istanbul and Perth.[23]

Tokyo remains a relatable example, but should not be described as the clear KrisFlyer sweet spot. Its value depends on personal upgrade willingness and the actual economy alternative. A lower award price to a destination you do not want is not a saving.

Istanbul business uses 40,500 fewer miles than Heathrow, approximately 37.3% fewer. This is a mileage comparison between different destinations, NOT an equivalent end-to-end London itinerary.[15]

## Recommendation for the five-minute talk
Keep Perth and Tokyo as the live exercise, Heathrow as backup. Add just one spoken line: “Some routes cost fewer miles—Istanbul business is 68,000—but only pursue a sweet spot if you actually want that trip.” Put detailed sweet spots and time-limited offers in the appendix, not another live slide.

## Preset integrity
Use full award miles, not the difference between economy and business award miles when comparing with buying economy in cash. Use actual itinerary economics, not half a return fare. Do not infer seat availability from a chart or promotion listing. Recheck source dates before presenting; expired promotional presets must be disabled.
