How to read the timeline: Transfer-rounded miles are earned from zero; accumulation is rounded up to whole months. Cashback and time costs cover every full earning month, including any end-of-month surplus. This is a simplified personal comparison, not financial advice. It assumes the hypothetical earn rate and one spend cap apply for every earning month. It does not model award-seat availability, expiry, devaluation, card eligibility, qualifying-spend requirements, statement timing, transaction exclusions, or taxes/fees not entered above.
